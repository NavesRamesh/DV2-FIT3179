05 — AGE AND CYCLING PARTICIPATION

Which age groups participate in cycling?

Explore the distribution of estimated adult cycling participants across age groups in Australia during the 2025–26 survey period.

Adults aged 55–64 have the highest estimated participation.

This age group accounts for approximately 375,000 estimated cycling participants, while the 45–54 age group is close behind.

Source: AusPlay Sport Data Tables, July 2025–June 2026. Note: These figures represent estimated adult cycling participation generally, not specifically recreational trail use.

06 — AGE-GROUP PARTICIPATION TRENDS

How Has Cycling Participation Changed Across Age Groups?

Compare adult cycling participation rates across seven age groups over three consecutive AusPlay survey periods. Discover how participation patterns differ between generations.

Older age groups show a different participation pattern.

Adults aged 55–64 increased from 11.73% to 12.10%, while those aged 65 and above increased from 5.53% to 5.77% across the three survey periods. In contrast, participation declined across several younger and middle-aged groups.

Source: AusPlay By Sport Data Tables, 2023–24, 2024–25 and 2025–26. Note: Figures represent estimated adult cycling participation generally, not specifically recreational trail use. Survey methodology changed in July 2023; this chart uses the post-change survey periods.
